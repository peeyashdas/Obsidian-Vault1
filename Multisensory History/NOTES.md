 Barthes considers photography as asymbolic, irreducible to the codes of language or culture, acting on the body as much as on the mind. The book develops the twin concepts of _studium_ and _punctum_: 
 - _studium_ denoting the cultural, linguistic, and political interpretation of a photograph,

### Sensory history : Auditory, Olfactory and Haptic

The 'embodied turn’ - 
**Affect Theory** and the senses - Affect Theory is a field that gained prominence around mid-90s, influenced by thinkers like Spinoza, Deleuze, Eve Sedgwick, and Brian Massumi. Affect theory is an approach to culture, history, sociality, and power that focuses on nonlinguistic forces, or affects. Affects make us what we are, but they are neither under our “conscious” control nor even necessarily within our awareness—and they can only sometimes be captured in language.

Although South Asian Studies has long been attentive to songs and lyrics as historical sources, it is only starting to interrogate the larger ambience of South Asian soundscapes with an attentiveness to how they shape social relations, politics and therefore history. Increasingly scholars and especially anthropologists are starting to question the idea of a ‘soundscape’ as an entity separable from other sensory input, with a new focus on ‘atmospheres’.
