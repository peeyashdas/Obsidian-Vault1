Major Approaches to Cultural Theory

- **Structuralism:** Looks for deep, underlying structures or universal rules in human thought and language. [[1](https://jcrelc.com/blog/key-theories-in-cultural-research-from-marxism-and-structuralism-to-postmodernism-and-ai), [2](https://www.studysmarter.co.uk/explanations/anthropology/east-asian-studies/cultural-theory/)]

- **Cultural Materialism:** Argues that a society's physical infrastructure (technology, economics, environment) shapes its social structure and cultural ideas. [[1](https://www.studysmarter.co.uk/explanations/anthropology/east-asian-studies/cultural-theory/)]

- **Post-Structuralism & Postcolonialism:** Questions fixed meanings, challenges Western-centric viewpoints, and highlights how language and institutions construct power (featuring theorists like Michel Foucault and Edward Said). [[1](https://www.encyclopedia.com/social-sciences/dictionaries-thesauruses-pictures-and-press-releases/cultural-theory), [2](https://jcrelc.com/blog/key-theories-in-cultural-research-from-marxism-and-structuralism-to-postmodernism-and-ai)]

- **Symbolic Interactionism:** Focuses on how people create and negotiate meaning through face-to-face interactions and everyday symbols. [[1](https://www.studysmarter.co.uk/explanations/anthropology/east-asian-studies/cultural-theory/), [2](https://jcrelc.com/blog/key-theories-in-cultural-research-from-marxism-and-structuralism-to-postmodernism-and-ai)]
