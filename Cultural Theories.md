Major Approaches to Cultural Theory

- **Structuralism:** Looks for deep, underlying structures or universal rules in human thought and language. 

- **Cultural Materialism:** Argues that a society's physical infrastructure (technology, economics, environment) shapes its social structure and cultural ideas.

- **Post-Structuralism & Postcolonialism:** Questions fixed meanings, challenges Western-centric viewpoints, and highlights how language and institutions construct power (featuring theorists like Michel Foucault and Edward Said). 

- **Symbolic Interactionism:** Focuses on how people create and negotiate meaning through face-to-face interactions and everyday symbols.
