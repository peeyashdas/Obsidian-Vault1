#### The Legacy of Romanticism

Romanticism comes in revolt of the rationality of the enlightenment. Germany (Jena) is the centre of Romanticism - all of the disenchantment was also emotionally unsatisfying - which also turns out to be a problem with liberalism.

Keywords: passion, beauty, longing , creation , will, 

Reaction / rebellion / revolt against hard sciences - rejection of the notion that all questions can be answered, that all virtues are compatible with each other ---- turn towards the preciousness of the individual soul and that the truth cannot be discovered through empirical experimentation but is an inner state of the soul.

#### Hegel and the Historicist Chronotope

*Phenomenology of Spirit* 

Comes with a reconciliation between enlightenment and romanticism - focus on overcoming of contradictions.
"Hegel is peculiarly seductive" - Marci Shore

What is *Geist* ?
Jay Burstein says when you say spirit , think team spirit. Hegel is going to take the idea of the collective social life and dignify it into an absolute philosophical ground. He is going to try to provide an account of the relationship between individual agency and the structures of social life. He talks about spirit the way other philosophers talk about God.  For Hegel, the Geist is the power of the whole and everything is relational.
For Hegel, alienation is the failure to understand your individual existence in the context of the who Geist - to fail to be one with this *telos*

Phenomenology of Spirit is the *bildungsroman* of the consciousness. 

*The owl of Minerva spreads its wings only with the falling of the dusk.* Meaning only comes with retrospect. This, is also Hegel's polemic with Kantian morality. 

**Hegelian Dialectics**
*Aufheben* - literally, to lift up; to transcend, to abolish but not cancel, 

The Master and the Slave 
Dialectical clash between the oppressor and the oppressed, eventually leading the slave to a dialectical inversion, where the master is the slave to the slave. 
- The idea of recognition - the master needs to be recognized as masters by the slave - only a subject can provide recognition
